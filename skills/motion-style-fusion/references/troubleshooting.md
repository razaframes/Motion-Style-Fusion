# Troubleshooting bad generations

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Color codes, sizes or odd words printed on screen | hex/px/% or font names were in the prompt | rewrite in plain words; run the linter |
| Letters garbled or misspelled | too much text, long words, text during fast motion | shorten to 8 words or fewer, hold text still for 2 s, one text element at a time |
| Liquid, melting or stretching shapes | morph-style words, many objects, many cuts | fewer beats, one hero object, solid rigid wording, slower motion |
| Objects multiply or drift | crowd wording, long shot | cap at 6 objects, shorten to 6-8 s, split into shots |
| Style differs from shot to shot | STYLE sentence reworded each time | paste the locked STYLE sentence identically |
| Continuation shot jumps | last frame not attached, or last frame was mid-motion | attach the exact last frame; end lead shot on a still hold |
| Voice or music appears | sound line vague | keep the exact "No music. No voiceover ... spoken word." wording and an AVOID line |
| Cuts feel random | too many beats | max 2-3 beats, with holds |
