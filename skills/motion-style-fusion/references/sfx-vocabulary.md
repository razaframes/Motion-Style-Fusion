# SFX vocabulary (sound effects only; never music, never voice)

## From analysis to prompt words
| sfx_events label | Say in prompt |
|---|---|
| deep thud / bass hit | a deep soft thud |
| deep boom with tail | a deep boom that fades out |
| short click / tick / snap | a tiny crisp click |
| soft pop / thump | a soft pop |
| airy whoosh / swish | an airy whoosh |
| rising whoosh | a rising whoosh |
| falling swish | a quick falling swish |
| accent hit with short tail | a short bright accent hit |

## Visual event -> typical SFX (when audio is missing or unclear; mark inferred)
hard cut -> thud plus quick swish / text reveal -> airy whoosh or soft pop / object lands -> soft thump / cursor click -> tiny crisp click / coin or metal -> coin clink / paper -> paper rustle or slide / counter ticking -> rapid tick loop / clock -> steady tick / big number appears -> deep boom / shapes snap into place -> crisp snap.

## Writing the SOUND line
`SOUND: Sound effects only: 0.3s soft pop as the box lands; 1.0s airy whoosh as the ring circles; 4.5s soft thump when the paper lands. No music. No voiceover, narration, speech, or singing - not a single spoken word.`
Every effect has a time and a cause. Do not mention tempo, melody, ambience beds that sound like music, or "background sound".
