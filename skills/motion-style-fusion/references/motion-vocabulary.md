# Motion vocabulary: smooth vs risky

## Safe verbs (use these)
slides in from the left / rises from the bottom / drops in and lands with one small bounce / grows from small to full size / fades in from slightly blurred to sharp / wipes in from left to right / slowly pushes in / rotates slowly in place / a hard cut to the next scene / holds perfectly still.

## Risky (rewrite)
| Risky | Why it breaks | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| morph, turns into, transforms into | model invents in-between shapes | cut or cross-fade to the new object, or slide the old out and the new in |
| liquid, melt, ripple, wobble, swirl | produces fluid warping | solid object sliding or scaling |
| breaks into dots/particles, explodes into | uncontrolled particle chaos | the object slides out, the next slides in |
| dozens/hundreds/grid of 40 | duplicates drift and merge | at most 6 clearly separated objects |
| text rearranges into other text | letters scramble | the old text fades out, new text wipes in |
| objects pass through each other | clipping artifacts | keep objects apart or stacked in order |
| fast whip pans, zoom plus rotate together | blur and warp | one slow camera move at a time, or locked |
| several things moving at once | attention and geometry smear | one main motion per beat, others still |
| long continuous transformations | drift from the original | split into separate shots |

## Rhythm
Fewer, longer beats. Let each motion finish, then hold a beat before the next. End every shot on a still frame.
